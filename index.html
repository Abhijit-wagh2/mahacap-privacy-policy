# Privacy Policy — MahaCAP Saathi **Effective date:** 25 October 2026 MahaCAP Saathi is an independent student-support application developed by Aashvi IT. It helps students explore Maharashtra Centralized Admission Process (CAP) information, admission resources, college prediction tools, and counselling guidance. MahaCAP Saathi is not affiliated with or endorsed by Maharashtra State CET Cell or the Government of Maharashtra. ## 1. Information We Handle Depending on the features you use, the app may handle: - **Account information:** Name, email address, mobile number, selected course or stream, and password. - **Admission planning information:** Saved college shortlists, preferences, checklists, and progress. - **App preferences:** Theme and text-size settings. - **Support interactions:** Questions entered into the Saathi chatbot. ## 2. How Information Is Used Information is used to provide account access, personalize the student experience, save admission plans, display relevant course information, and help users navigate the app. ## 3. Data Storage and Sharing The app is designed to store account information and saved data locally on the device. The current design does not provide cloud account synchronization between devices. We do not sell users' personal information. Any data transmission or sharing with third-party services, if present, must be disclosed accurately here. ## 4. Passwords and Security Users should protect their devices and account credentials. The app developer takes reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. ## 5. Third-Party Websites The app may link to official CET Cell websites, admission portals, and other external resources. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Users may use the account deletion option available in the app, where supported, to delete their locally stored account information. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data. If any information is stored or processed outside the device, the applicable retention and deletion procedures will be disclosed here. ## 7. Children's Privacy The app is intended to support students exploring admission opportunities. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in ways prohibited by applicable law. Where parental consent or additional safeguards are required, they must be implemented before the relevant data is collected. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change.
